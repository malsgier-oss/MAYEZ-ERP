# MAYEZ-ERP — Staff Quick Guide

**One-page reference.** Print or save as PDF.

---

## 🛒 POS (Make a sale)

1. Open **POS** (first menu item).
2. **Search** or tap products to add to cart. Change quantity with + / −.
3. Tap **Select customer** → choose a customer or **Walk-in**.
4. Optional: add **Discount** (amount or %).
5. Tap **Cash** (paid now) or **Credit** (adds to customer debt).
6. Sale is done. Receipt can be printed from **Invoices** if needed.

---

## 📄 Invoices

- **Invoices** → list of all sales. Filter by date or status.
- Open an invoice to **Print**, **Record payment**, or **Process return**.

---

## 💰 Record payment (customer pays later)

1. **Invoices** → open the unpaid invoice.
2. Click **Record payment**.
3. Enter amount → **Record**.
4. Repeat until balance is zero if needed.

---

## ↩️ Process return

1. **Invoices** → open a **paid** or **partial** invoice.
2. Click **Process return**.
3. Tick the items being returned → choose **Reason** → **Confirm return**.
4. Stock is restored automatically.

---

## 📊 Inventory (fix stock)

1. **Inventory** → see all products and stock.
2. Use **Low stock** filter to see items below threshold.
3. Click **Adjust** on a product → enter new quantity → choose reason (e.g. Counted, Damaged) → Save.

---

## 📈 Reports

- **Reports** → daily sales, customer debt, low stock, top products.
- Pick date range if needed.

---

## ⚙️ Settings

- **Settings** → business name, address, currency, receipt footer. Click **Save settings**.

---

## Need help?

- **Products / Customers:** Use **Products** and **Customers** in the menu to add or edit.
- If something fails: check internet; try again; tell the owner.
